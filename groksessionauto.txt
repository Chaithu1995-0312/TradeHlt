DOMAIN REASONING

In a market, four different questions get mixed because people say “strategy” for all of them:

1. What is the market doing? Range, sweep of liquidity, impulse away from the taken side, expansion, come-back (retest). That is structure.
2. When are we willing to act? Session, filters, “only after a retest.” That is policy.
3. If we act, where is the trade? Entry, stop, target, time-to-live. That is execution geometry.
4. May we put risk on? Size, RR after costs, vetoes. That is risk.

A strategy is (2) applied to (1), then (3) and (4).
A structure detector is only (1).
If you treat (1) as a strategy, every valid pattern looks like a trade. The repo’s own constitution says the opposite: structure validity ≠ execution validity.

CRT (Candle Range Theory / ICT-style) is a standard domain picture of (1): a range is raided, price displaces away from the raid, expands, then returns. That is market meaning. It is not automatically “go long/short now.”
