Declared capital and XAUUSD sizing inputs

In v2_htfcrt_2026_08.json: backtest.initial_capital = ₹100,000, risk_pct_per_trade = 0.01, use_compounding = true (598–600); execution_planner.risk_percent = 1.0 (71); capital_management.usd_to_inr_rate = 84.0 (783). XAUUSD declares contract_size = 100, lot_step = 0.01, lot_min = 0.01, lot_max = 10.0 (803–806). These are repository settings, not a current exchange-rate quote or a measured live account balance.

INR risk budget → divide by USD/INR rate → divide by stop distance → divide by contract size → floor to lot_step → reject below lot_min / cap at lot_max. Authority: position_sizing.py:size_trade_lots (94–147).

Illustration only: ₹100,000 × 1% = ₹1,000 risk budget. With a hypothetical $5 stop distance, the declared rate 84 and 100 oz/lot give approximately 0.02381 raw lots, floored to 0.02 lots. Stop-loss exposure is then ₹840 before costs. No single fixed lot size follows from account capital alone; stop distance and the broker contract also determine it.

Per-trade risk capital is the loss budget at the stop. Account investment is the total balance basis. Margin and position notional are separate quantities; this section reports the risk-sizing inputs and outputs, not a broker margin allocation.
