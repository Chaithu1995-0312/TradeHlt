State Machine

The M15 execution plane is a 9-state deterministic finite automaton driven by CRTEngine.process_candle() and StateMachine._transition(). The 3 parent-timeframe states (RANGE_C1, MANIPULATION_C2, DISTRIBUTION_C3) form a disjoint sub-graph driven by ParentCRTTrack; they never transition into / from the 9 execution states.

Authority Lanes

Lane Defines / drives Primary source
Engine Runtime M15 execution states: RANGE through RESOLUTION. src/config_layer/crt_engine_v2.py imports CRTState and VALID_TRANSITIONS from state_identity.py.
Resolver Shadow/declarative M15 state resolution for overlays, parity work, and diagnostics. src/features/crt_state_resolver.py reads configs/formulas/market_crt_states.yaml.
Parent Track Parent-timeframe C1/C2/C3 states: RANGE_C1, MANIPULATION_C2, DISTRIBUTION_C3. src/config_layer/parent_crt.py drives ParentCRTTrack; the legal states and edges still come from state_identity.py.
