Reached 1R before the stop: each pattern against all bars with the same stop size

A wide stop gives price more room, so a raw hit rate mostly reflects how far away the stop sat. Each pattern is compared with every bar whose stop was the same size (in ATR), not with random bars. The dot is the pattern, with its 95% range; the ring is all bars of the same stop size.
