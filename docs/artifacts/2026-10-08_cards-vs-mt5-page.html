Where they agree, where they part

Agree

  • Structure (08, 09), levels (10) and fair value gaps (11) are all measured on the page with the same definitions the cards draw.
  • Card 12's "strong momentum candle" shows up in the data. Over 47,197 bars, a winning run starts on a bar with a bigger body (body_ratio 0.58 vs 0.46 long) and higher volatility (1.20 vs 0.90).

Part ways

  • The cards say: see the pattern, then trade. The page tests that. With a 1-ATR stop and 2-ATR target over 2 years, every bar nets −0.020R long and −0.118R short after the $0.26/oz cost, with win rates of 29.2% and 27.4%.
  • Entering on the features that mark zone starts does not fix that. They describe a strong move that already began.
  • The card shows one BOS or HH event. The page keeps the flag on for many bars, so "BOS = 1" means "recently broke", not "breaking now".

Missing from the page

  • The pattern features from cards 03–07 and the inside bar (hammer, shooting star, doji, engulfing, pin bar, inside bar). The repo added them on 8 Oct (schema 8.0), and the working tree is now at schema 9.0 with 79 features (morning star, higher low, lower high, sideways and rejection-block features added, not yet committed); the page has the older 48.
  • The clock is MT5 broker time. Since 8 Oct the repo converts corpora to UTC once on load, so the page's session and hour columns are on the old basis.

Teaching material, not evidence. The cards name what you see. The MT5 page and the repo's findings (F-086, F-097, and the 8 Oct profitable-zone study) have not found that these names pick winning trades on gold.

What would bring the MT5 page in line

Rebuilding the page itself is not done (it needs a live MT5 terminal for the 10-day window). Rebuild it from the current pipeline so it carries the pattern columns, with times through the corpus loader in UTC. The pattern "Does it pay?" test is done separately: Candle Pattern Pay Test. Over two years, none of the nine directional patterns beat random bars on the side the cards name.

Every number here was read from the MT5 page's own embedded data (meta, rows, smc_z, amd, zones_full, zone_edge). The hammer and pin-bar thresholds come from feature_pipeline.candle_patterns in the active config and src/features/candle_patterns.py.
