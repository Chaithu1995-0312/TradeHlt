Candle theory, one card at a time

A short animated walk through the reference cards: from one candle, to market structure, to a planned trade. Each scene redraws a card in plain English and names the feature in this repository that measures the same thing.

Teaching material, not evidence. These patterns have not been shown to give an edge on XAUUSD. The repo's measured results say the opposite so far: F-086 (the feature and state vocabulary does not mark a profitable entry bar), F-097 (no context family adds expectancy beyond CRT state alone), and the 2026-10-08 profitable-zone study (entering on zone-start features loses money after cost). Use the cards to name what you see, never as the reason a trade will work.
