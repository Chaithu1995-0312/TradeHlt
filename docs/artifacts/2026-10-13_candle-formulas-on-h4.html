Candle Formulas on H4

Candle formulas on XAUUSD H4

Covers 30 of the 79 feature-schema slots (v9.0): the 21 candle-shape slots, plus the volatility and momentum groups below (atr, volatility_ratio, volatility_regime, momentum_score, rsi_14, MACD line, signal, histogram and its z-score), and 7 candle-anatomy register entries that are not slots.

Each register formula drawn on a real XAUUSD 4-hour bar from data/mt5/XAUUSD_H4.csv. Pattern cards show a bar the pattern fires on; intensity cards show a bar with a non-zero value. Every card shows both sources side by side: values recomputed from the drawn OHLC, and the feature pipeline's own value for the same bar, with ✓ where they agree. Below that, each condition is re-derived from OHLC (✓ = holds). The ATR size gate and the 5-bar swing need data not drawn here, so on those cards the OHLC side covers the drawn conditions only.

Data caveats. This H4 file has no declared clock in configs/data_provenance/ohlcv_clock_registry.json (timezone empty, not reviewed; the detector says it looks UTC, low confidence), so bar times are shown as written in the file. The dataset registry lists it as a forensic file, not the canonical corpus.

bullish body (close ≥ open) bearish body (close < open) bar the formula is evaluated on

The feature pipeline's windows are bar counts tuned for M15. On H4 bars the 5-bar swing and the 14-bar ATR therefore cover about four times as much time. Wick shares, body shares and the two-bar patterns use only the bars drawn, so they are unaffected.
