Candle Pattern Pay Test
XAUUSD M15 · 2-year corpus · 47,197 bars · UTC

Does a candle pattern pay on its own?

The cards say a pattern is the weakest read without a level and a confirming candle. This tests that: enter on the pattern bar alone, with the same stop, target and cost the MT5 bar-features page used, and compare against random bars of the same count.

R is in units of the stop (1 ATR) after the $0.26/oz cost. "vs random" is the share of 2,000 same-size random-bar draws that did at least as well; a pattern that beats luck would show a small value. Halves are the first and second year.

How it was run

  • Data. data/mt5/XAUUSD_M15.csv, 47,275 rows loaded through the corpus loader (times converted to UTC), 78 warm-up bars dropped, 47,197 bars tested, 22 May 2024 to 21 May 2026.
  • Patterns. The repo's own definitions from src/features/candle_patterns.py, computed by the feature pipeline, so a flag at bar t uses only bars up to t. Thresholds are the active config's feature_pipeline.candle_patterns block.
  • Trade. Enter at the close of the flagged bar. Stop 1 ATR, target 2 ATR, 16 bars. A bar that touches both counts as a stop. A trade that times out is marked to market at bar 16. Cost $0.26/oz round trip, the same fixed figure the MT5 page used. This is the rule from tools/xau_chart/zone_edge.py, unchanged.
  • Control. For each row, 2,000 random sets of the same number of bars drawn from all bars; "vs random" is the one-sided share at least as good.
  • Side. Hammer, pin_lower, engulfing_bull, dragonfly_doji and morning_star are tested long; shooting_star, pin_upper, engulfing_bear and gravestone_doji short. doji_material and inside_bar have no direction, so both sides are shown. The opposite side of each directional pattern is in the table as a check.
