Features exist on M15 only (that is the pipeline's timeframe); on other timeframes the pane still shows the M15 series on the same clock. EMA overlays and the warm-up marker are drawn on M15.

SMC monitor (M15)

Each value is tanh(signed distance from close to the zone's near edge ÷ ATR), from features/smc/*. Near 0 = price at the zone edge; ±1 = far away (about 3.8 ATR or more). Positive = price on the zone's favourable side (above a bullish zone, below a bearish one). 0 is also emitted when no active zone exists, so 0 alone cannot tell "touching" from "none". The ATR column converts back with atanh. HH and LL rows use the M15 bar where the window's highest high and lowest low printed.

Oracle trades: sequential, largest TP

How each trade is built (hindsight, uses future bars on purpose). Enter at a bar's close. Look at every later bar to the end of the window. TP = the furthest price reached in the trade's direction (first bar to reach it). SL = the tightest stop that would not have been touched up to and including that TP bar (a bar touching both counts as stopped), never on the wrong side of entry, minus one tick. Keep the trade only if TP distance minus the round-trip cost is above zero; otherwise try the next bar. The next trade starts at the close of the TP bar. Long and short run as two separate chains. Cost $/oz round trip, from the repo's measured XAUUSD broker calibration (F-082): half-spread, commission and entry slippage in, half-spread and commission out. Overnight swap is not charged because it has not been measured. Net per lot = 100 oz.

Feature ranges: low→high vs high→low moves

Moves are the oracle trades above: long trades are the low→high legs, short trades the high→low legs. Each bar shows the feature's middle half (25th–75th percentile, thick) and full range (thin) on a scale of that feature's own min–max over the whole window; black = up moves, grey = down moves. Separated means the two middle halves do not overlap. Sorted by the gap between medians in units of the pooled spread. Moves that start inside the warm-up have no feature values and are left out. These are hindsight legs and a handful of samples: a separation here describes these ten days and is not evidence that a feature predicts direction.
