## The pattern (confirms the thesis)

The spine consumes **~6** signals (CRT, Gaussian, RR, ZoneGate-geometry, LLM, StrategyOrchestrator).
**At least 5 built artifacts are sidecar / stub / log-only:** zone expectancy, TradeNet, ReplayMemory,
MarketStateCluster/HMF, and concept-drift. The repeated shape is **"produced & stored → spine ignores it."**
The single strongest piece of evidence is `fusion_engine.py:8`: there is a **designated adaptive slot in
fusion that is explicitly stubbed** ("plug TradeNet GGUF here").

## Highest-ROI "connect what exists" candidates (vs inventing new)

1. **Concept drift is detected but ignored** (`live_engine_hook.py:676`). Today HARD drift logs
   "Trade signal unreliable" and the trade proceeds anyway. Wiring drift → size-down/skip is a small,
   high-leverage **Category-A** change (it protects live money) — and it's the most defensible "is the
   edge still alive?" hook, cheaper than the Probability Surface.
2. **Zone expectancy is stored but the gate is purely geometric.** Whether feeding `mean_rr` into the
   zone gate (as a weight/filter) is a *missed lever* or *intentional separation* is a design call —
   but the data is already there, unused.
3. **The TradeNet fusion stub** is a pre-built socket; the model is trained but never plugged in.
4. **ReplayMemory advisory** (already sub-planned) — the deterministic-path version.

## Caveats
